Chess Rules

Players: 2

Board: 8×8 squares

Goal: Checkmate

1. What is Chess?

Chess is a strategic board game played between two players. One player controls the white pieces, the other controls the black pieces. The game is played on a square board divided into 64 squares, alternating light and dark colors.

2. The Chessboard

The chessboard has 8 rows (ranks) and 8 columns (files). Each player starts with pieces arranged on the first two ranks. The bottom-right square for each player must always be white.

3. Chess Pieces

  • King – the most important piece
  • Queen – the strongest piece
  • Rook – moves in straight lines
  • Bishop – moves diagonally
  • Knight – moves in an “L” shape
  • Pawn – moves forward, captures diagonally

4. Movement of the Pieces

King: Moves one square in any direction.

Queen: Moves any number of squares in any direction.

Rook: Moves horizontally or vertically.

Bishop: Moves diagonally.

Knight: Moves two squares in one direction and one square perpendicular. Can jump over pieces.

Pawn: Moves forward one square (two squares on its first move). Captures diagonally.

5. Special Moves

Castling:
A move involving the king and a rook. Conditions:

  • Neither piece has moved before
  • No pieces between them
  • The king is not in check
  • The king does not pass through check

En Passant:
A special pawn capture that can occur immediately after a pawn moves two squares forward.

Pawn Promotion:
When a pawn reaches the last rank, it is promoted to a queen, rook, bishop, or knight.

6. Check, Checkmate and Draw

Check:
The king is under attack and must be protected.

Checkmate:
The king is in check and cannot escape. The game ends.

Draw:
A game can end in a draw by:

  • Stalemate
  • Threefold repetition
  • 50-move rule
  • Insufficient material
  • Agreement between players

7. Illegal Moves

  • Leaving your king in check
  • Moving a piece through another piece (except knight)
  • Castling through check

8. Winning the Game

You win a chess game by checkmating your opponent’s king or if your opponent resigns.
